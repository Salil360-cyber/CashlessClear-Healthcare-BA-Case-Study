# 1. Problem statement and goals

## Background

Narmada Hospitals (fictional) runs three multispeciality hospitals in Nagpur, Pune and Nashik. Most insured patients use **cashless** treatment: the hospital's insurance desk asks the insurer (directly or through a TPA, a Third Party Administrator) to approve the treatment at admission (**pre-authorisation**), and to approve the final bill at discharge (**final approval**). The patient can leave only after final approval and paying any amount the insurer does not cover.

IRDAI's Master Circular on Health Insurance (May 2024) asks insurers to decide on cashless requests **within 1 hour** and to give final discharge approval **within 3 hours** of the hospital's request. The hospital uses these timelines as its targets.

## Problem statement

> From January to June 2026, only **50.5%** of 12,000 cashless pre-authorisations were decided within 1 hour, and only **66.8%** of final approvals came within 3 hours. On average a patient waited **7.6 hours** from the doctor's discharge order to leaving the hospital, and **2,508** patients were kept an **extra night**. More than a third of the wait (**2.85 hours, 37.6%**) is the hospital's own time to prepare the discharge file, and most insurer queries ask for documents the hospital already has (missing investigation reports, incomplete discharge summaries, missing bill breakups). Patients with retail policies in single rooms also face surprise room-rent deductions of **24.1%** of the bill. This affects patients and families, doctors, the insurance and billing desks, bed availability, and the hospital's reputation.

## Business goals (SMART)

1. Cut average discharge order-to-exit time from **7.6 hours to under 6 hours** within 6 months of launch.
2. Cut extra bed-nights caused by discharge delay from **2,508 to under 1,900** per six months.
3. Raise pre-authorisations decided within 1 hour from **50.5% to at least 65%** by cutting insurer queries.

## Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-authorisation and discharge document checklists | Changing insurer or TPA processes and policies |
| Discharge planning (morning discharge orders) | Hospital tariffs and package rates |
| Insurance desk tracker, escalation and MIS | Reimbursement claims filed by patients |
| Room-rent eligibility check at admission | Clinical treatment decisions |
| NHCX (National Health Claims Exchange) connection, next phase | Hospital information system replacement |
